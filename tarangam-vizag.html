The Chef's Famous Dish

One dish, one story — the plate every regular orders on their first visit.

RT
Head Chef · Ravi Teja Ponnada

Royyala Iguru (Andhra-style Prawn Roast)

₹420 · serves 2 · fiery

Chef Ravi Teja's grandmother ran a fish stall two streets from Jagadamba Centre — this recipe is hers, tightened up over eleven years on the line. Prawns are seared hot and fast, then finished in a thick onion-tomato masala built on stone-ground guntur chilli and curry leaf. It's the dish that put Tarangam on the map, and it hasn't left the menu since day one.
